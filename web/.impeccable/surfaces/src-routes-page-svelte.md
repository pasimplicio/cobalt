Scope: whole web app shell (sidebar, save page, dialogs, settings, about). Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience: Brazilian users saving media from a pasted link, on phone or desktop. Job: paste, save, understand failures.
Constraints: brand pinned by logopsdev.png (navy #001B48 P, electric blue #006DFF S, `</>`); theme follows system; PT-BR default; cobalt credit kept on About; no cobalt name, logo or mascot.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The link box is a lit console, the way the logo's blue S glows out of navy. Refuses cobalt's grey monospace minimalism and the generic white SaaS card.

OWN-WORLD: Navy rail (#0A1633) in both themes, electric blue (#006DFF) for the one live thing at a time: selected tab, selected mode, valid-link download button, focus. Cool ink-blue neutrals, never pure black or white. Sora for all UI text, mono only for filenames/data. 12px radius everywhere, pills only for segmented switchers.

STORY: Visitor sees the PSDev mark and one input, pastes, the box lights up, the download button fills blue, the file saves. Errors speak plain Portuguese with a recovery.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy rail left (bottom bar on phones) with the PS monogram on top. Centered column: full PSDev logo lockup at ~240px wide, then the 640px link box with Auto/Áudio/Sem som and Colar below, terms note at the bottom.

FORM: Brand-pinned direction (user-provided logo is binding); Operate mode, code-led. No concept-seed roll: brief pinned the world. Signature move: focus/valid-link glow on the omnibox in electric blue, with the download button turning into a filled blue key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
